END-TO-END WORKFLOW

How a log becomes a decision

Transparent, auditable progression from raw bytes to analyst-approved containment.

  1. 01
    Ingest Any log file: syslog, Windows EVTX, Zeek, Suricata, CEF, CSV, JSON and more, plus simulations and this machine's own activity.
  2. 02
    Normalize Maps heterogeneous inputs to an unified schema with timestamps, entity IDs, and preserved raw payloads.
  3. 03
    Detect IOA rules mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, threat intelligence matching, statistical baselines and two ML models.
  4. 04
    Correlate Temporal entity linkage clusters isolated alerts into multi-stage attack chains and incident graphs.
  5. 05
    Score & Explain Severity, confidence and a risk score from five factors, each with a plain-English reason.
  6. 06
    Respond & Alert Small risks are handled automatically. High and critical ones wait for SOC approval, and alert emails go out.
SOC CAPABILITIES

Engineered for high-fidelity security operations

Automated ATT&CK Mapping

Every detection rule is mapped directly to MITRE ATT&CK tactics and techniques (T1110, T1078, T1059, T1041, T1021), providing instant tactical posture visibility.

Hybrid ML Detection Ensemble

A LightGBM, CatBoost and XGBoost ensemble on flow data, and an Isolation Forest for unusual activity. Their results stay suspicious until a rule or threat intelligence supports them.

Instant SMTP Alert Engine

Real-time email alerts dispatched over encrypted SMTP (TLS/STARTTLS) directly to on-call analysts with full incident context, evidence summaries and direct links.

Analyst Session Management

Role-based access control for Analysts and Auditors. Every triage update, response approval, and configuration adjustment is recorded in an immutable audit ledger.

NARS Security Copilot

A read-only assistant that answers from stored data, with a Groq model or locally. Ask about incidents, suspect IPs, threat parameters and impact, and see what it read.

Append-Only Audit Ledger

Database triggers refuse changes and deletions to the audit log, so that every analyst decision and automatic step stays on record.

INTEGRATED EMAIL NOTIFICATIONS

Automated SMTP Incident Alerting

When an incident reaches HIGH or CRITICAL risk, ALLSAFE's dedicated background worker constructs a structured security advisory and dispatches it over authenticated SMTP.

  • Encrypted Dispatch: Supports standard STARTTLS (port 587) and SSL/TLS (port 465).
  • Forensic Summary: Contains rule IDs, ATT&CK tags, affected assets, and pending actions.
  • Deep-Link Navigation: 1-click jump straight to the incident timeline in the SOC Console.
  • Storm Protection: Configurable hourly caps prevent inbox flooding during heavy attack simulations.
